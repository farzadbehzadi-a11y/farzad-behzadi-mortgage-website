Property

Documents about the home you are buying, refinancing or already own.

Property

New Home Warranty Certificate

What it is
Proof that a newly built home is enrolled in home warranty coverage. In BC this is typically 2-5-10 coverage: two years on materials and labour, five on the building envelope and ten on the structure. Lenders want to see it before funding a new build.
Where to get it
Your builder or developer issues it at completion.

Property

Title Search

What it is
The official land title record for the property: who owns it, its legal description, and any mortgages, liens or other charges registered against it.
Where to get it
Your realtor often includes one with the listing. Your lawyer or notary can order one, and in BC it can be pulled through the Land Title and Survey Authority.
Go to LTSA (opens in a new tab)

Property

Strata Documents

What it is
The paperwork for a condo or townhouse: bylaws and rules, recent meeting minutes, financial statements, the budget and the depreciation report. Lenders look for special levies, legal action or major repairs the building can’t pay for.
Where to get it
The seller or listing realtor. Otherwise the strata management company, usually for a fee.

Property

Purchase Waiver

What it is
The signed notice that removes your conditions (subjects) from an accepted offer, such as financing or inspection. Once it is signed, the purchase is firm.
Where to get it
Prepared and signed with your realtor, once your financing is confirmed.

Property

Purchase Agreement

What it is
The complete signed contract of purchase and sale, including every schedule, addendum and amendment. It sets the price, deposit, subject removal date and completion date the lender works to.
Where to get it
Your realtor.

Property

Property Tax Notice

What it is
The annual property tax bill for a home you own. It confirms the yearly tax amount, which is part of how lenders calculate what you can afford.
Where to get it
Mailed by your city or municipality each year. Most also show it in an online property tax account.

Property

Property Tax Certificate

What it is
An official statement from the municipality of the current taxes on a property and anything still owing. Often needed on a refinance to show there are no arrears.
Where to get it
Requested from the municipality’s tax office for a small fee. Your lawyer or notary can also order it.

Property

Property Disclosure Statement

What it is
The seller’s written account of what they know about the property’s condition, such as past leaks, renovations, permits or pests.
Where to get it
The seller provides it through the listing realtor.

Property

Mortgage Statement

What it is
A recent statement for an existing mortgage, showing the lender, balance, payment, interest rate and maturity date. Needed for refinances, renewals and switches, and for any property you already own.
Where to get it
Your current lender’s online banking, or the annual statement they send you.

Property

MLS Listing

What it is
The listing sheet for the property: asking price, size, age, taxes, strata fees and features. It gives the lender a first look at the home.
Where to get it
Your realtor.

Property

Home Inspection

What it is
A licensed inspector’s report on the condition of the home. Some lenders ask for it, particularly on older homes or private sales.
Where to get it
The home inspector you hire before removing subjects.

Property

Final Builder Statement of Adjustments

What it is
The builder’s closing statement on a new build or presale. It shows the final price, deposits already paid, GST and rebates, and the balance due on completion.
Where to get it
Your builder, usually sent through your lawyer or notary shortly before completion.

Property

Condo Board Letter

What it is
A letter from the strata confirming the monthly fees, any special levies and that the unit is in good standing. In BC this is usually the Form B Information Certificate.
Where to get it
The strata management company or the strata council.

Property

Appraisal

What it is
An independent valuation of the property. The lender uses it to confirm the home is worth enough to support the mortgage.
Where to get it
Ordered through the lender or your mortgage professional once the application is underway. You don’t need to arrange it yourself.

Income

Documents that confirm what you earn, whether you are salaried, self-employed, retired or have other income.

Income

Letter of Employment

What it is
A letter on company letterhead confirming your position, start date, whether you are full or part time, and your salary or hourly rate and guaranteed hours. Lenders want it signed by a manager or HR and dated within the last 30 days.
Where to get it
Your employer’s HR or payroll team.

Income

Pay Stub

What it is
A recent pay stub showing your employer, pay period, gross pay and year-to-date earnings. Usually the most recent one, dated within 30 days.
Where to get it
Your employer’s payroll or HR portal.

Income

Final Pay Stub Calendar Year

What it is
Your last pay stub of the year, showing total earnings for the full year. It helps confirm income that varies, such as overtime, shift premiums or bonuses.
Where to get it
Your employer’s payroll or HR portal.

Income

T4

What it is
The Statement of Remuneration Paid. It shows your employment income and deductions for the year.
Where to get it
Your employer issues it by the end of February. It also appears in CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

Notice of Assessment

What it is
CRA’s assessment after you file your tax return. It confirms the income you reported and whether you owe anything. Most lenders ask for the last two years.
Where to get it
CRA My Account, or the copy CRA mails you after you file.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T1 General

What it is
Your full personal income tax return. Usually required for the last two years if you are self-employed, earn commission or have rental income.
Where to get it
Your accountant or tax software. A summary is also available in CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T1 Statement of Business Activities

What it is
Form T2125, the part of your personal return that reports self-employment income and expenses. Lenders use it to calculate qualifying self-employed income.
Where to get it
Included in your T1 return. Ask your accountant or check your tax software.

Income

T1 Statement of Rental Income

What it is
Form T776, the part of your personal return that reports rental income and expenses for properties you own.
Where to get it
Included in your T1 return. Ask your accountant or check your tax software.

Income

6-Months Business Bank Statements

What it is
Six months of statements for your business account, showing regular deposits. Used to support stated income for self-employed borrowers.
Where to get it
Your bank’s online banking. Download the PDF statements rather than screenshots.

Income

T2 Corporate Income Tax Return

What it is
The full tax return for your corporation, with schedules. Lenders usually want two years to understand an incorporated borrower’s income.
Where to get it
Your accountant, or CRA My Business Account.
Go to CRA My Business Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

Corporate Financial Statements

What it is
Your company’s balance sheet and income statement, prepared by your accountant. Lenders usually ask for the last two years.
Where to get it
Your accountant.

Income

Audited Financial Statements

What it is
Financial statements independently audited by a CPA. Usually only required for larger corporations.
Where to get it
Your accountant or auditor.

Income

Articles of Incorporation

What it is
The documents that show your company exists and who owns it, such as the Certificate of Incorporation and Notice of Articles.
Where to get it
Your corporate records book or lawyer. BC companies can also be searched on BC Registries.
Go to BC Registries (opens in a new tab)

Income

Business License

What it is
Your municipal business licence. It helps show the business is active and how long it has operated.
Where to get it
Your city or municipality’s business licensing office.

Income

CRA Statement of Account

What it is
A statement of any balance you owe CRA. Lenders ask for it when a Notice of Assessment shows an amount owing, to confirm it has been paid.
Where to get it
CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T4A

What it is
A slip for pension, retirement, annuity and other income, such as an employer pension or some self-employed commissions.
Where to get it
Whoever paid you issues it. It also appears in CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T4A(P)

What it is
The slip for Canada Pension Plan (CPP) benefits you received during the year.
Where to get it
My Service Canada Account, or the copy mailed to you.
Go to My Service Canada Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T4A(OAS)

What it is
The slip for Old Age Security (OAS) benefits you received during the year.
Where to get it
My Service Canada Account, or the copy mailed to you.
Go to My Service Canada Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T4E

What it is
The slip for Employment Insurance benefits, including maternity and parental benefits.
Where to get it
My Service Canada Account, or the copy mailed to you.
Go to My Service Canada Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T4RSP

What it is
A slip showing money withdrawn from an RRSP during the year.
Where to get it
The institution that holds your RRSP. It also appears in CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T5 Statement of Investment Income

What it is
A slip showing interest, dividends and other investment income for the year.
Where to get it
Your bank or investment firm. It also appears in CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

T3 Trust Income

What it is
A slip showing income allocated to you from a trust or mutual fund.
Where to get it
The trust or investment firm. It also appears in CRA My Account.
Go to CRA My Account (opens in a new tab)

Income

Letter from Pension Provider

What it is
A letter confirming your monthly or annual pension amount and that it continues for life or a set term.
Where to get it
Your pension plan administrator.

Income

Disability Insurance Letter

What it is
A letter confirming the amount and expected length of long-term disability benefits.
Where to get it
Your insurer or benefits provider.

Income

Lease Agreements

What it is
Signed leases for rental properties you own, showing the rent and the term. Used to count rental income toward qualifying.
Where to get it
Your own records or your property manager.

Income

Canada Child Benefit Notice

What it is
CRA’s notice showing your monthly Canada Child Benefit. Some lenders count it as income while your children are young enough to receive it.
Where to get it
CRA My Account.
Go to Canada.ca (opens in a new tab)

Income

Separation Agreement

What it is
The signed agreement setting out support payments and how property is divided. Lenders need it whether you pay support or receive it, and when a home is being transferred.
Where to get it
Your family lawyer or mediator.

Down payment

Documents that show where your down payment and closing costs are coming from.

Down payment

90-Day Bank Statement on All Accounts

What it is
Ninety days of history for every account holding your down payment, showing your name and account number. Any large deposit in that period will need to be explained.
Where to get it
Your bank’s online banking. Download PDF statements rather than screenshots.

Down payment

Bank Statement Showing Source of Deposit

What it is
A statement that shows where a large deposit came from, such as a transfer from another account or the proceeds of a sale.
Where to get it
Online banking for the account the money came from.

Down payment

RRSP Statement

What it is
A recent statement for an RRSP you plan to use, for example through the Home Buyers’ Plan.
Where to get it
The institution that holds your RRSP.

Down payment

TFSA or FHSA Statement

What it is
A recent statement for a TFSA or FHSA you plan to use toward the purchase.
Where to get it
The institution that holds the account.

Down payment

Investment Statements

What it is
Recent statements for non-registered investments you plan to use, such as stocks or mutual funds.
Where to get it
Your investment firm or online brokerage.

Down payment

Sale Agreement

What it is
If your down payment comes from selling a property, the accepted contract of purchase and sale for that property, with subjects removed.
Where to get it
Your realtor.

Down payment

Realtor Deposit Receipt

What it is
A receipt confirming the deposit you paid on your offer was received into the brokerage’s trust account.
Where to get it
Your realtor or their brokerage.

Down payment

Gift Letter

What it is
A signed letter from an immediate family member confirming the money is a gift and does not have to be repaid. Many lenders require their own form, listed under Lender forms.
Where to get it
Your mortgage professional provides the right template. You and the person giving the gift both sign it.

Down payment

Confirmation of Gift Deposit

What it is
Proof the gift has arrived: a statement showing the deposit in your account. Some lenders also ask for the giver’s statement showing it leaving theirs.
Where to get it
Online banking.

Down payment

Line of Credit Statement

What it is
A recent statement showing the limit and balance of a line of credit, especially if it is funding part of the purchase.
Where to get it
Online banking.

Down payment

Bank Draft

What it is
A guaranteed payment issued by your bank, often used for a deposit or closing funds. A copy shows the money came from your own account.
Where to get it
Your bank branch.

Credit

Documents that support your credit history, including if you are new to Canada.

Credit

12-Months Confirmation of Rent Payments

What it is
Twelve months of bank statements or cancelled cheques showing on-time rent payments. Useful if you are building credit or are new to Canada.
Where to get it
Online banking.

Credit

Proof of US Credit

What it is
A credit report from the United States, if you have lived or borrowed there.
Where to get it
The US credit bureaus. Free reports are available through AnnualCreditReport.com.
Go to AnnualCreditReport.com (opens in a new tab)

Credit

Proof of Foreign Credit

What it is
A credit report or bank reference letter from the country you lived in before Canada. Documents not in English may need a certified translation.
Where to get it
A credit bureau or your bank in that country.

Credit

Proof of Collections Paid

What it is
Confirmation that a debt in collections has been paid: a letter from the collection agency, or a statement from the creditor showing a zero balance.
Where to get it
The collection agency or the original creditor.

Credit

Proof of Alternative Credit

What it is
For borrowers with little Canadian credit history: around 12 months of on-time payments for rent, utilities, phone or insurance.
Where to get it
Statements from each provider, or online banking showing the payments.

ID

Government identification for everyone on the application. Copies must be clear, in colour and not expired.

ID

Driver’s Licence

What it is
Government-issued photo ID. Send the front and back, and make sure it hasn’t expired.
Where to get it
ICBC in BC, or a registry agent in Alberta.
Go to ICBC (opens in a new tab)

ID

Passport

What it is
The photo page of a valid passport, as primary photo ID.
Where to get it
Passport Canada, if you need to apply for or renew one. Allow plenty of time.
Go to Canada.ca (opens in a new tab)

ID

Permanent Resident Card

What it is
Your Permanent Resident card, front and back. It confirms your status in Canada, which affects which programs you qualify for.
Where to get it
Issued by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.
Go to IRCC (opens in a new tab)

ID

Health Card

What it is
Accepted as a second piece of ID by some lenders. In BC this is the BC Services Card.
Where to get it
Most people already carry one. New BC residents enrol through MSP.
Go to Gov. of BC (opens in a new tab)

ID

Void Cheque

What it is
A voided cheque or a pre-authorized debit form from your bank. It sets up your mortgage payments to come out of the right account.
Where to get it
Write VOID across a cheque, or download a direct deposit / pre-authorized debit form from online banking.
